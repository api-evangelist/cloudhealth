Modern enterprise environments demand uncompromising visibility across management boundaries. In VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF) 9.1, audit logging undergoes a major evolution with standardized Audit Records and the Audit Trail framework. Together, these capabilities bring unified logging standards, centralized analysis, and deep cross-component traceability to combat unauthorized changes, lateral movement, and insider threats.
